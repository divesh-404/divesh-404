## About Me

Solo founder of **[HUMAN](https://www.joinhuman.in/)**, a production campus social app — and currently deep in **AI infra**, platform engineering, and distributed systems. I care about the layer between products and models: queues, reliability, and services that survive real traffic.

- Currently working on **[NEXUS](https://github.com/divesh-404/NEXUS)** — an AI orchestration platform (intelligent infra between companies and the models they use), plus AI infra & distributed systems
- Learning **AI infra & platform engineering** — Docker, Redis, queues, observability, model-serving patterns, FastAPI services
- Looking to collaborate on **backend systems, AI infra, distributed queues & realtime apps**
- Ask me about **FastAPI, Next.js + Supabase, Redis, production backends, DSA**
- Reach me at **[diveshkumavat0@gmail.com](mailto:diveshkumavat0@gmail.com)**
- Fun fact: *Those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it* ~ dynamic programming (DP)
- 2026 goals: ship stronger AI-infra / platform work, deepen distributed systems, keep shipping production systems
